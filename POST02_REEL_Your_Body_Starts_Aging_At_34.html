What changes at 34
Muscle synthesis declines — without training, you lose 3–5% of muscle mass per decade from here
Metabolic rate slows — fat accumulates even without dietary change
Collagen drops 1%/year — skin joint and arterial wall integrity begins declining
GH output falls — growth hormone declines 14% per decade from peak

What changes at 60
Cardiovascular aging accelerates — arterial stiffness and plaque accumulation compound sharply
Immune senescence — aging immune system begins attacking healthy tissue
Cognitive risk measurable — Alzheimer's amyloid accumulation detectable 20 years before symptoms
Hormone floor reached — testosterone DHEA and estrogen at significantly reduced baselines
